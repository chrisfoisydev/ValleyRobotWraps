Our Process

From 3D Scan to Fleet Rollout

Humanoids aren't vans. Compound curves, articulated joints, thermal vents, and sensor arrays demand a wrap process built for robots from the ground up.

  1. 1

    Scan & Template

    We 3D-scan one unit of your model (or use our verified Optimus Gen 2/Gen 3 templates) to map every panel, joint, and no-wrap zone.

  2. 2

    Design Proof

    Our designers place your logo, colors, role titles, and unit ID system on a 3D proof you approve before anything is printed.

  3. 3

    Print & Precision Cut

    Cast vinyl rated for actuator heat and daily flexing is printed and plotter-cut to panel-perfect tolerances.

  4. 4

    Certified Install

    Optimus-certified installers wrap each unit in our facility or on-site — sensors masked, vents clear, joints free. About 48 hours per unit.

  5. 5

    Fleet Rollout & Care

    We stagger installs so your line never stops, register every unit ID, and back it all with a 3-year wrap warranty and free panel repairs.

FAQ

Questions Fleet Managers Ask

Will a wrap void the Optimus warranty or interfere with operation?

No. We use removable cast vinyl applied only to approved exterior panels. Sensor windows, cameras, thermal vents, joint boots, and charging contacts are templated as no-wrap zones. Wraps remove cleanly with no adhesive residue.

How long does a wrap last on a working robot?

Rated for 5 years of daily duty cycles. High-flex zones (elbows, hips) use a stretch-rated film and are covered by our free panel-repair program for the warranty period.

Can you wrap on-site so our fleet keeps working?

Yes. For fleets of 10+ we send a certified install crew and wrap in rotating batches — typically 4–6 units out of service at a time — so throughput never drops.

Can each robot get a unique name and ID?

That's our specialty. Every unit can carry an individual nametag, unit number, role title, and scannable QR asset code — all managed in a fleet manifest we hand back to your ops team.

What happens when we rebrand or resell units?

We remove or re-skin. Removal restores the factory finish; re-skins reuse your existing templates, so a rebrand costs a fraction of the original wrap.
